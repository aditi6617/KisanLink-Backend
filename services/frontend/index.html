⚙️ How KisanLink Works

1️⃣ Enter Details

Select your crop and enter the quantity you want to sell.

2️⃣ Detect Location

GPS is used to determine the farmer's location.

3️⃣ Compare Markets

KisanLink compares market prices, distance and selling costs.

4️⃣ Calculate Net Realisation

The expected return is calculated after relevant costs.

5️⃣ Find Best Strategy

The system identifies the most profitable feasible market option.

6️⃣ Smart Split Selling

When useful, the quantity can be distributed across multiple markets.

🌾 KisanLink Key Features

📍 GPS-Based Analysis

Uses the farmer's current location for market-distance analysis.

💹 Price Comparison

Compares available market prices instead of considering price alone.

💰 Net Realisation

Estimates the actual expected return after selling-related costs.

📦 Partial Selling

Supports allocation of produce across up to three feasible markets.

🔄 Previous Sale Analysis

Uses the farmer's previous selling outcome to provide rule-based insights.

📊 Visual Insights

Charts make market performance and selling allocation easier to understand.

ℹ️ Prototype Note: Market prices, distances, capacities and cost values depend on the available backend data. Net realisation represents an expected calculation and may differ from the farmer's actual final sale outcome.
